Call for papers

Co-creation and co-existence with AI in the e-business ecosystem

ICEC 2027 invites research on how people and AI can work well together in electronic commerce, and on how that partnership can make the e-business ecosystem more sustainable.

  • Building harmony between humans and AI in e-business contexts
  • Supporting a more sustainable e-business ecosystem

Topics include, but are not limited to

  • Human–AI collaboration in e-commerce
  • AI and consumer experience
  • Generative AI in marketing and content creation
  • AI-driven decision-making and automation
  • Trust, ethics, and responsible AI
  • Human creativity and AI co-creation
  • AI and the future of digital work
  • AI-powered platforms and innovation
  • Data privacy and security in AI ecosystems
  • Sustainable and inclusive AI in e-commerce

Submission

Full papers, short papers, and student research are welcome. Paper formats, page limits, and the submission system link will be posted here.
